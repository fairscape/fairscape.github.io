FAIRSCAPE GUI Client

Overview

The FAIRSCAPE Electron App provides a user-friendly interface for packaging research objects and validating metadata, making it easier to ensure your research data is FAIR (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, and Reusable).

📦

RO-Crate Creation

Create Research Object Crates (RO-Crates) to package your research data with its associated metadata through an intuitive graphical interface.

📝

Metadata Management

Register and manage metadata for your research objects through user-friendly forms. Simplify the process of maintaining comprehensive documentation.

🔄

Guided Workflow

Navigate through the process of creating, packaging, and uploading your research objects with our step-by-step guided interface.

Installation Instructions

1

Go to the Releases page

2

Download and run the latest version for your operating system (Windows, macOS, or Linux)

3

Note: You may have to open the application on Mac from a finder window due to Mac permissioning rules.
